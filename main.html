Cryptographic Integrity

Immutable Ledger Guaranteeing Zero Fertilizer Diversion

Every allocation run and physical pickup is cryptographically sealed into a SHA-256 blockchain block. Once registered, records cannot be altered, forged, or claimed by unauthorized proxies.

Cryptographic Block Linking

Each block cryptographically references the hash of the preceding block.

NIN & Biometric Anti-Fraud

Restricts allocations to one verified farmer per National Identity Number.

Rural SMS Token Integration

Farmers without smartphones receive cryptographic 6-digit SMS tokens.
